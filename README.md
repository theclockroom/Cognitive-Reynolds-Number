Weekly self-assessment and tracking of media scenarios. Bilingual (Chinese / English).

A single-file web app (`index.html`): zero dependencies, no account, no upload. All data stays in your browser's local storage.

## 它做什么 · What It Does

对每周在新闻、搜索、短视频、社交、AI 对话等媒介场景中的信息接触做自报记录，按明示参数估算各场景的信息到达压力（λ）与核查安排之间的关系，输出认知雷诺数方向性提示与周报（可导出 Markdown）。

Record weekly self-reports of information exposure across media scenarios (news, search, short video, social feeds, AI chat); estimate the relation between information-arrival pressure (λ) and verification arrangements under explicit parameters; get directional hints and an exportable weekly report (Markdown).

- 本软件不是医疗器械或医疗软件，不提供任何医学测量、诊断、治疗或健康建议；也不是心理测验，不做任何心理、精神或成瘾状态的判定。
- 全部数值均为明示参数下的费米估算（情景估算），不是精确测量；参数与规则可能随版本更新调整，历史记录不保证跨版本可比。
- 输出只给方向性提示与下一步动作，不构成对认知能力、心理状态或健康状况的任何判断。
- 数据全部保存在本设备浏览器本地：不上传、不收集、无账号。清理浏览器数据会删除记录，请定期导出备份。

- This software is not a medical device and provides no medical measurement, diagnosis, treatment or health advice; nor is it a psychological test.
- All numbers are Fermi estimates under explicit parameters, not measurements; parameters and rules may change across versions.
- Outputs are directional hints and next actions only.
- Data is stored locally in your browser; nothing is uploaded. Clearing browser data deletes records — export backups regularly.
